Balakrishna (Balayya) and Jr NTR are two powerhouse actors from the legendary Nandamuri family in Telugu cinema; Balayya, son of NTR, is known for his mass appeal, fiery dialogue delivery, and a cult following with iconic action dramas, while Jr NTR, NTR’s grandson, has carved his own space with extraordinary acting versatility, unmatched dance skills, and global fame through RRR; together they represent two generations of Tollywood stardom, carrying forward the Nandamuri legacy in their own unique styles.
